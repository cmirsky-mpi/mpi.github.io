About MP Industries

At MP Industries, our goal has always been to provide customers with excellent molded rubber oilfield products. For more than 45 years, we have been doing just that. From packer parts, stripper rubbers, sucker rod guides, valve parts and even seals, we continue to be the leaders in the production of oilfield mechanical molded rubber products!
